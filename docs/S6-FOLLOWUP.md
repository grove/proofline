## What changed

Production reviewer/prover guidance now derives source clauses and domains before trusting implementation tests, challenges inherited library/operator restrictions, requires discriminating evidence with independent expectations, and treats unresolved material coverage as `NOT VERIFIED`. A qualifier describing narrower coverage cannot justify a positive verdict about the complete promise. No benchmark answers, new assurance stage or weaker acceptance rule were added. **This prompt remedy failed its native trust controls.**

The native transport puts the selected interpreter on PATH, permitting short exact command citations. Implementer/reviewer guidance recommends describing checks without optional command citations; prover citations remain mandatory and exact. Unavailable or invalid handoffs still fail closed.

The P2P adapter now binds Git to its actual execution repository on every invocation, including after changing directory. Its host-created shim lives outside the candidate and is read-only to workers. Login shells cannot reset PATH. The denied executions ancestor, sibling executions, operator oracle and network restrictions remain in place. The actual controller and its stage packages remain responsible for delivery.

The evaluator/reference fixes preserve all twenty original source promises. They cover Unicode White_Space versus Python whitespace predicates; additional ISO date representations, midnight and year padding; unrestricted sortable pagination scores; conditional ID comparison; malformed singleton rows; and non-boolean integer subclasses through the public library. Existing large-CSV and long-version-component probes remain enabled. These are evaluator improvements, not proof that production verification became trustworthy.

`--followup` freezes twenty exposed regression tasks plus four newly authored service requests: transactional document synchronization, dependency jobs with retries/cancellation, warehouse reservations, and recurring bookings with atomic overlap checks and exact billing. The services provide several starting modules and leave implementation architecture and tests to the worker. They are larger synthetic tasks, not production repositories or deployment evidence. The old component tasks cannot be described as fresh held-out tasks.

## Limits and next decision

The failed source-domain controls remain unresolved production verification failures. Native isolation and command binding validate who ran what against which candidate; they do not validate the model's coverage inference. Prompt changes alone have demonstrably failed. Evaluator improvements also uncovered defective positive references, so independent ground truth needs its own stronger coverage justification.

**Redesign verification; do not expand the efficiency claim.** A future design must establish source-derived domain coverage without importing candidate restrictions, then demonstrate both rejection and positive acceptance before another frozen comparison. This follow-up does not add a speculative stage or repeatedly restart a scored campaign until favorable. A new design and campaign would need new unexposed tasks, genuinely representative larger repositories, prospectively measured operator effort and frozen budgets/prices when available. The present stop leaves E1–E3 and the user's complete S6 comparison explicitly incomplete.
