pass.** A reviewer must inventory *all material source obligations* independently
of these proposals, adjudicate unsupported assumptions and semantic ambiguity,
allow materially distinct correct solutions (not only equivalent line rewrites),
and construct real defective and green-suite-omission controls. A source quote
only pins provenance; it does not prove entailment or adequate coverage.
